# Submitting reimbursement claims and receipts

Submit a reimbursement claim through Benefits > Claims > New Claim.
A paid, itemized receipt must show the provider, purchase date, description, and amount paid.
Choose the expense category and attach the receipt before submitting.
The applicable reimbursement policy determines eligibility, limits, and receipt deadlines.
A submitted confirmation means the claim was received; it does not mean the expense was approved.
Check Benefits > Claims for claim status. This knowledge base contains definitions, not an employee's personal claim records.
