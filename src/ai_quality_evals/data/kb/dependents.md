# Adding eligible dependents

An employee may add a spouse, a registered domestic partner, or a child under age 26 as a dependent.
To add a spouse, upload a marriage certificate. To add a domestic partner, upload a registration certificate.
To add a child, upload a birth certificate or adoption order.
Dependent verification documents must be uploaded within 30 calendar days of the enrollment request.
Adding a dependent outside annual enrollment also requires a qualifying life event; the life-event reporting deadline is separate from verification.
