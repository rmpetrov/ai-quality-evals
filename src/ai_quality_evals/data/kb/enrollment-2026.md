# Annual enrollment window for 2026

Annual enrollment runs from November 2 through November 20, 2026.
Changes selected during annual enrollment take effect on January 1, 2027.
Use the Benefits > Enrollment page to submit selections before 11:59 p.m. Eastern time on the closing date.
Outside annual enrollment, only a new-hire enrollment or a qualifying life event permits changes.
This policy replaces the archived 2025 annual enrollment window; those older dates do not apply in 2026.
