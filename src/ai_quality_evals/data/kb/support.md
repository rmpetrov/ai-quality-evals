# Benefits support and urgent coverage escalation

Open a support request through Benefits > Help and include the claim reference when relevant.
If a claim remains In review for more than five business days, request a status escalation through Benefits > Help.
For an urgent loss-of-coverage concern, choose Priority: Urgent coverage.
The benefits support team aims to acknowledge urgent coverage requests within one business day.
This acknowledgement target is not a claim payment guarantee or a promise that an appeal will be approved.
