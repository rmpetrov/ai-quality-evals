# Medical plan deductible and out-of-pocket limit

The medical plan has a $1,500 individual annual deductible for in-network care.
The individual annual in-network out-of-pocket limit is $4,500.
These amounts apply to medical benefits only. The dental annual maximum is a separate limit.
The knowledge base does not list procedure-specific medical coverage, individual premiums, or out-of-network reimbursement amounts.
