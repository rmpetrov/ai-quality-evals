# Wellness reimbursement policy for 2026

Wellness reimbursement is limited to $300 per employee per calendar year.
Eligible expenses include gym memberships and instructor-led fitness classes.
Home exercise equipment, nutritional supplements, and commuting expenses are not eligible.
Submit a paid, itemized receipt within 90 calendar days of the purchase date.
Unused wellness allowance does not carry forward.
This current policy replaces the archived 2025 wellness policy and its lower reimbursement cap.
