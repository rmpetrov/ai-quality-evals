# Commuter transit reimbursement

Commuter reimbursement is limited to $120 per employee per calendar month.
Eligible expenses are public-transit passes used for travel between home and work.
Parking fees, fuel, and gym memberships are not eligible commuter expenses.
Submit a paid transit receipt within 60 calendar days of purchase.
Unused monthly commuter allowance does not carry forward.
Commuter and wellness reimbursements have separate limits and receipt deadlines.
