# Employee eligibility and new-hire coverage

Regular employees scheduled for at least 20 hours per week are eligible for benefits.
Contractors and temporary employees are not eligible under this policy.
New hires must complete a 60-day waiting period.
Coverage starts on the first day of the month following completion of the waiting period.
New hires may submit enrollment selections during the waiting period using the Benefits > Enrollment page.
The annual enrollment closing date does not cancel a new hire's enrollment opportunity.
