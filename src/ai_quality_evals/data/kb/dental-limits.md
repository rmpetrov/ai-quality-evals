# Dental annual maximum and preventive care

The dental plan pays up to $1,500 per covered person per calendar year.
Preventive dental exams and cleanings have no deductible.
The dental annual maximum is separate from the medical deductible and medical out-of-pocket limit.
Unused dental benefits do not carry forward into the next calendar year.
