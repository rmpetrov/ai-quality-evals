# Qualifying life events and reporting deadlines

Marriage, birth, adoption, and loss of other health coverage are qualifying life events.
Report a qualifying life event through Benefits > Life Events within 30 calendar days of the event.
Attach evidence of the event and select the affected benefit elections.
Buying a house or changing a mailing address is not a qualifying life event.
A qualifying life event may allow enrollment changes outside the annual enrollment window.
Dependent verification follows the separate adding-dependents policy.
