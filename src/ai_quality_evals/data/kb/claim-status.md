# Claim status terminology

Submitted means the claim was received and has not yet been reviewed.
In review means the claim is being checked; additional information may be requested.
Approved means review is complete and reimbursement is scheduled; it does not mean funds have arrived.
Paid means reimbursement has been issued.
Denied means the claim was rejected; the decision notice gives the reason and appeal instructions.
These are general definitions. The knowledge base cannot determine a specific employee's current claim status.
