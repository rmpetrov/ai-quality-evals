# Archived wellness reimbursement policy for 2025

ARCHIVED: this historical wellness policy is superseded by wellness-2026.
Wellness reimbursement was limited to $200 per employee per calendar year in 2025.
Gym memberships required paid receipts submitted within 60 calendar days of purchase.
These archived limits and receipt deadlines do not apply to purchases in 2026.
