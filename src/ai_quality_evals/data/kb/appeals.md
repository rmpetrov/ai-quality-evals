# Appealing denied reimbursement claims

An employee may appeal a denied reimbursement claim within 30 calendar days of the decision notice.
Use Benefits > Claims > Appeal and attach the decision notice plus supporting receipts or documentation.
Do not create a duplicate initial claim to appeal a denial.
The support escalation process does not replace the appeal submission deadline.
